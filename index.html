ABOUT KINEO

Kineo (kineo.live and the Kineo mobile application) is an AI-powered text-to-cartoon video creation platform built to simplify storytelling for modern content creators. Traditionally, producing animated videos required expensive software, high-end hardware, and weeks of complex frame-by-frame editing. Kineo eliminates these barriers by turning simple text prompts into high-quality, monetizable cartoon videos within minutes—allowing creators to focus entirely on their ideas rather than technical hurdles.

Our original vision was to make Kineo 100% free for every creator. However, running advanced AI video rendering models requires dedicated cloud infrastructure and continuous server maintenance. To keep the platform sustainable while still making it accessible to students, independent creators, and storytellers everywhere, we offer a special ₹100 lifetime access offer within the first 24 hours of downloading the app.

  • Built for Creators, Not Editors: Skip complex timelines and manual keyframing—generate complete animated stories directly from a single text prompt.

  • Privacy-First Architecture: Your prompts are cleared immediately after generation, and videos download straight to your device's local gallery with zero server storage.
